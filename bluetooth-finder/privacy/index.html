Privacy Policy — Bluetooth Finder: Find Earbuds

Effective date: November 28, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Bluetooth Finder: Find Earbuds ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our Android application.

1. Local Bluetooth & Device Scanning (On-Device Only)

  • Bluetooth & BLE Signals (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): The App scans for nearby Bluetooth and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) signals solely to calculate real-time signal strength (RSSI), estimate proximity, read publicly broadcast or GATT battery telemetry, play acoustic locator tones on connected audio devices, and maintain a local disconnect log on your screen.
  • No Server Upload: All scanned Bluetooth device names, identifiers, signal readings, battery snapshots, saved device labels, and disconnect timestamps are processed and stored exclusively locally on your device. We do not collect, upload, or share your Bluetooth environment data with any server or third party.
  • No Location Collection on Android 12+: On Android 12 (API level 31) and higher, the App uses BLUETOOTH_SCAN with the neverForLocation flag and does not request ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION. On Android 11 (API level 30) and lower, the Android operating system requires location permission solely to deliver BLE scan callbacks; the App never reads, records, or transmits your GPS coordinates.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party SDKs

The App integrates standard third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and in-app purchases:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & Mediation Partners: May collect your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), Advertising ID (AD_ID), App Set ID, device model, OS version, and ad interaction events for serving ads (personalized or non-personalized based on your consent), analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): Collects anonymous installation identifiers, crash stack traces, diagnostic metrics, and basic app interaction events to improve stability.
  • Google Play Billing: All subscription and one-time purchases are processed directly by Google Play. We do not have access to your credit card or banking details.

3. Consent & Privacy Options (GDPR / UK / US States)

Users in the EEA, the UK, and applicable US jurisdictions are presented with a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog on startup. You can modify your privacy choices at any time via Settings → Privacy Options inside the App, or reset your Advertising ID in your Android system settings.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not maintain user accounts or store personal data on our own servers, you can erase all locally stored app data (saved devices and disconnect history) at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

5. Children

The App is intended for general audiences (18+) and is not directed to children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at bebrasoftai@gmail.com.
