1. Local Sensor, Camera & Network Scanning (On-Device Only)

  • Camera Permission (CAMERA): Used exclusively in real time on your device's screen to apply high-contrast optical color filters and control the flashlight torch so you can visually inspect a room for lens reflections. The App does not record, store, or transmit photos or video streams from the optical detector.
  • Local Network Inspection (INTERNET, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE): When you run the Wi-Fi Scanner, the App communicates locally with your current router subnet (via TCP, mDNS, SSDP, and ONVIF) to list active devices on your screen. Your local network topology and IP list are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Magnetometer & Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Magnetic sensor readings (μT) and nearby Bluetooth Low Energy signal checks are processed strictly in memory on your handset.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party SDKs

The App uses standard third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and billing:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & Mediation Networks: May collect your IP address (to estimate approximate location), Advertising ID (AD_ID), App Set ID, device information, and ad interaction events to display advertisements, measure performance, and prevent fraud.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): Collects crash logs, anonymous installation IDs, and diagnostic metrics to keep the App stable.
  • Google Play Billing: Handles all subscription and one-time Pro purchases securely through Google Play.

3. Links to Our Other Apps

The App may show a link to our other apps (such as Danger Radar). Tapping it opens that app if it is installed, or its Google Play page. Nothing is passed to it except a referral marker (utm_source); no scan results, location or personal data are shared.

4. Consent & Privacy Options (GDPR / UK / US States)

Users in the EEA, the UK, and applicable US jurisdictions are presented with a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog on startup. You can modify your privacy choices at any time via Settings → Privacy Options inside the App, or reset your Advertising ID in your Android system settings.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not operate user accounts or store personal user data on our own servers. You can clear all local scan logs and preferences at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
