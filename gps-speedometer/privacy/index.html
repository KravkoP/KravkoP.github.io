Privacy Policy — GPS Speedometer

App: GPS Speedometer (Android)
Effective date: November 17, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how GPS Speedometer ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Android application.

1. Location

  • The App needs your precise location to measure speed, distance, altitude and heading. Without the permission the App cannot work.
  • Your location is processed on your device and is not sent to us or to any server of ours.
  • While you record a trip, the App can keep a foreground service with a visible notification (type "location") so the recording continues when the screen is off. You can turn it off in Settings → Automation and background. The App does not read your location when no trip is being recorded and the App is closed.
  • Trips (route, speed, distance), drag-test results and settings are stored on your device. You can delete them in the App, by clearing the App's data, or by uninstalling.
  • GPX, CSV and PDF exports and "Share" send data only to the app you choose in the Android share sheet.

2. Information collected by third-party SDKs

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and mediation partners may collect your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), Advertising ID, App Set ID, device and app information and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads, for analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) collects app usage events, installation identifiers, crash reports and diagnostics to improve the App.
  • Google Play Billing processes subscriptions and one-time purchases. We do not receive your payment details.

Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Consent and privacy options

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and applicable US states, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy and ad choices in the App.

4. Backup

If Android backup is turned on, your device may include the App's settings and trips in its backup to your Google account or when you move to a new device; Google manages that backup and we cannot access it. We have no user accounts and no servers that hold your personal data.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: bebrasoftai@gmail.com
