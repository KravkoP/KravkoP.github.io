Privacy Policy

Astro AI Horoscope: Palm Tarot (Android app, package com.bebrasoftai.palmreader). Effective date: October 23, 2026. Русская версия

This privacy policy explains how Astro AI Horoscope: Palm Tarot ("the app", "we", "our") handles your information. The app is developed by BebraSoftAI. Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com.

Overview

We respect your privacy. The app does not require creating an account. Most of your data stays entirely on your device.

Camera & Palm Photo Data

The app offers an optional palm scanning feature to provide palmistry line readings. You can scan your palm using your device camera or choose an image from your gallery.

  • Camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA) is requested only when opening the scanner. It is used strictly in real-time to capture the image of your palm.
  • To perform the AI analysis, the palm image is transmitted securely over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our backend powered by Google Gemini AI.
  • The photo is processed once to generate the palm reading traits and is not stored or shared for any other purpose.

Information on Your Device

The following information stays on your device in local storage and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Your name, gender, and birth date/time entered during onboarding (used to calculate your astrological chart and horoscope).
  • Your past readings, saved tarot cards, horoscope history, and wheel of fate progress.
  • Your audio and display preferences.

You can delete all local data at any time by clearing the app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services, Advertising & Analytics

Advertising: The free version of the app displays ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver, personalize (where consented), and measure ads, AdMob and its advertising partners collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address (approximate location), and diagnostic data.

Analytics and Performance: We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage and improve app stability. Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymous crash reports to help us resolve technical issues.

All data transmitted to third-party services is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

We offer optional Premium subscriptions (monthly and yearly) via Google Play In-App Billing. Google Play handles all billing and payment details securely. We do not receive or store any credit card or financial information.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
