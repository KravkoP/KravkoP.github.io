Privacy Policy

AI Palm Reader Horoscope Tarot (Android app, package com.bebrasoftai.palmreader). Effective date: November 5, 2026. Русская версия

This privacy policy explains how AI Palm Reader Horoscope Tarot ("the app", "we", "our") handles your information. The app is developed by BebraSoftAI. Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com.

Overview

We respect your privacy. The app does not require creating an account. Most of your data stays on your device. The AI features send data to our server and to Google, as described below.

Camera & Palm Photo Data

The app offers an optional palm scanning feature to provide palmistry line readings. You can scan your palm using your device camera or choose an image from your gallery.

  • Camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA) is requested only when opening the scanner. It is used strictly in real-time to capture the image of your palm.
  • To perform the AI analysis, the palm image is sent as described in "AI Features: Palm Reading & Chat". Your copy of the photo stays on your device.

AI Features: Palm Reading & Chat

Palm reading and the astrologer chat are AI features. Data leaves your device only when you use them.

  • Palm reading sends the photo, the hand (left or right), the app language, and your first name, gender and zodiac sign.
  • The chat sends your recent messages and the replies in that conversation, the chosen character, the app language, and your first name, gender and zodiac sign.
  • This data goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server on Cloudflare Workers, which passes it to the Google Gemini API and returns the answer. Our server does not save your photo or messages.
  • We use the paid Gemini API. Google states that it does not use prompts or responses from paid services to improve its products. Google logs them for a limited time to detect misuse and for safety, security and legal reasons, and may store them transiently or cache them in any country where Google or its agents have facilities. See the Gemini API terms.
  • Our server and Cloudflare use your IP address and the country it indicates to limit abusive requests and to apply regional availability. We do not save them.
  • Please do not enter health, financial or other sensitive details in the chat.
  • The Gemini API is not offered in some countries. There the AI features do not work and the app tells you so. Your request stops at our server and does not reach Google.

Information on Your Device

The following information is kept on your device in local storage. Apart from the details listed under "AI Features: Palm Reading & Chat", it is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Your name, gender, and birth date/time entered during onboarding (used to calculate your astrological chart and horoscope).
  • Your past readings, saved tarot cards, horoscope history, and wheel of fate progress.
  • Your audio and display preferences.

You can delete all local data at any time by clearing the app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services, Advertising & Analytics

Advertising: The free version of the app displays ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver, personalize (where consented), and measure ads, AdMob and its advertising partners collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address (approximate location), and diagnostic data.

Analytics and Performance: We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage and improve app stability. Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymous crash reports to help us resolve technical issues.

All data transmitted to third-party services is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

We offer optional Premium subscriptions via Google Play In-App Billing. Google Play handles all billing and payment details securely. We do not receive or store any credit card or financial information.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
