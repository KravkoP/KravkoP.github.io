Privacy Policy — AI Cat Translator: Talk & Meow

Effective date: November 3, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

This policy explains how AI Cat Translator: Talk & Meow (shown on your phone as “Human to Cat Translator”), a prank and entertainment app by BebraSoftAI, handles information.

1. On-Device Features & Microphone

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): asked only when you tap the record button. The recording is kept in temporary device memory while it is analysed and is never saved to a file, stored, or uploaded.
  • On-device AI: the recording is analysed on your phone by Google's YAMNet sound-classification model (bundled inside the App). It only recognises the kind of sound, such as a meow, purr, hiss or caterwaul. No audio or analysis result leaves your device. The funny “translation” shown afterwards is a joke, not a real translation.
  • Sounds and games: Sounds, phrases and the laser and fish games run locally on your device.
  • History, favorites and settings: your translation history (the result only: mood, joke and recognised sound type, never audio), favorite sounds and phrases, and app settings are stored on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include them in your device backup to your own Google Drive; we cannot access that backup.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party SDKs

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & mediation partners: may collect your device's IP address (to infer approximate region), the Advertising ID (AD_ID), App Set ID, device technical information and ad interaction events to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): collects app-instance identifiers, crash reports, device and app version information and usage events (for example, that a translation was completed) to keep the App stable and improve it. No audio is sent.
  • Google Play Billing: the one-time “Remove ads” purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

3. Sharing

When you tap “Share” or “Invite a friend”, Android's share sheet opens with a short text and a Google Play link. The text goes only to the app and person you choose; we receive nothing.

4. Consent & Privacy Choices (GDPR / UK / US States)

Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and relevant US states see Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form. You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy settings inside the App, or reset your Advertising ID in your device settings.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We have no user accounts, profiles or servers with your data. You can clear the translation history in the History tab, and remove all app data by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
