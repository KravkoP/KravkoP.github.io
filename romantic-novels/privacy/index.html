Privacy Policy

Romantic Novels (Android app, package com.bebrasoftai.novels). Effective date: October 17, 2026. Русская версия

This policy explains what information the Romantic Novels app ("the app", "we") collects, why, and what choices you have. The app is developed by BebraSoftAI. Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com.

Who the app is for

The app contains romance stories for readers aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

No account, and what stays on your device

You do not need an account. The following is stored only on your device and is not sent to us:

  • the first name you may enter during setup (optional);
  • your reading progress, saved stories, finished stories and hero cards;
  • your keys, daily bonus, quests and key wheel state;
  • reader settings such as text size.

Deleting the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes all of it.

Information collected automatically

Advertising. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob and, through AdMob mediation, by AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, Liftoff (Vungle), Mintegral and Pangle. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these services collect and share the device's advertising ID and app set ID, the IP address (which can indicate approximate location), interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic information. Ads may be personalized only if you agree (see "Your choices"). Premium subscribers do not see ads, except rewarded videos they choose to watch.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used: an app instance identifier, events such as opening or finishing a chapter or watching a rewarded video, the hero types you pick during setup, device model, OS version, language and approximate country. We use it to improve the stories and the app; it is not used to identify you.

Crash reports. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports (stack traces, app state, device details and an installation identifier) so we can fix problems.

App configuration. Firebase Remote Config receives your country code, language, time zone, OS version and app version so the app can load its settings.

All of this is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Purchases

Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google; we never receive your card or payment details. The app receives the purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play to unlock Premium.

Sharing a quote

When you choose to share a moment from a story, the app opens Android's share sheet. What you share and with whom is up to you; the app does not see it.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent to personalized ads through Google's consent form when you first open it. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android settings (Privacy → Ads). Ads will then not be personalized.
  • Premium removes all ads except the videos you choose to watch.
  • Delete your data. Uninstall the app or clear its data: this deletes everything stored on the device and resets the app's analytics and crash identifiers. Analytics and crash data are not linked to your name, email or any account. For any other request about your data, write to bebrasoftai@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

Retention

Analytics data linked to an app instance is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, after which Google deletes them automatically. Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Write to bebrasoftai@gmail.com. We do not sell personal information for money; sharing with ad partners for advertising is described above, and you can opt out of personalized ads as described in "Your choices".

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
