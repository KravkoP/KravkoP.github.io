BebraSoftAI / Auto Clicker Privacy

Privacy Policy — Auto Clicker

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

1. Accessibility Service

Auto Clicker uses Android's Accessibility service API exclusively to perform the automated taps and swipes that you place, configure, and start yourself, and to display its floating control panel overlay.

  • No Screen Content Reading: The Accessibility service does not inspect or read screen content, text, messages, passwords, or personal data.
  • Zero Data Transmission: No data is collected, stored, or transmitted via the Accessibility service.
  • User Initiated: Gestures execute only upon your explicit command and stop immediately when you pause or close the panel.

2. Local Scenarios Storage

Your saved clicker scenarios (point coordinates, timing intervals, and sequence order) are stored strictly locally on your device within the app's sandboxed storage. They are never uploaded to any remote server and are completely erased upon app uninstallation.

3. Third-Party SDKs (Ads & Analytics)

The app integrates industry-standard third-party SDKs:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to serve ads in the free version. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID, approximate location (derived from IP address), and interaction metrics for ad personalization and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): Collects anonymous diagnostics, crash logs, and non-identifiable usage statistics to maintain app reliability.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are securely processed directly by Google Play. We do not receive or store any credit card or payment details.

4. Privacy Choices (GDPR & US States)

Users in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states can view and manage their ad privacy choices at any time via Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

5. Children

The app is not directed toward children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at bebrasoftai@gmail.com.
