1. On-Device Acoustic Cleaning & Audio Generation

  • Sound Generation & Vibration (VIBRATE): Generates specific high and low frequency acoustic sine wave tones and haptic vibration pulses to physically shake trapped water, dust, and lint out of your phone's speaker and earpiece grills.

  • Settings & Tests: Frequency profiles and speaker check results are saved only locally in device storage.
