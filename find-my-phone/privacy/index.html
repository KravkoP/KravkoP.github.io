Privacy Policy — Find My Phone Anti Theft Alarm

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Package: com.bebrasoftai.findmyphone
Developer: BebraSoftAI — bebrasoftai@gmail.com
Русская версия

This Privacy Policy explains how Find My Phone Anti Theft Alarm ("the app", "we", "our") handles your information. In short: no account is required, microphone audio and motion sensor data are processed solely on your device in real time and are never recorded or uploaded, while advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and purchases are handled by the third-party services described below.

Overview

The app does not require registration or personal accounts, and we do not operate our own servers to store your personal data. We do not sell your personal data for money.

Audio & Microphone Processing

To detect claps and whistles when searching for your phone, the app requests Microphone permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) and runs a microphone foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE) while clap/whistle detection is active.

  • On-Device Ephemeral Processing: Audio captured by the microphone is processed strictly in real time in the device's volatile memory (RAM) via an on-device fast Fourier transform (FFT) algorithm.
  • Zero Recording or Transmission: The app does not record, save, store, or transmit any audio, voice, or sound recordings. Raw audio frames are discarded immediately after spectral analysis.

Device Sensors & Anti-Theft Guard

The Anti-Theft feature uses the device accelerometer and charging state to detect unexpected movement or unplugging from the charger. Sensor readings are evaluated locally on your device and never leave it.

Data Kept on Your Device

Your sensitivity preferences, chosen alert sounds, quiet hours, language selection, lucky wheel spins, and ad frequency counters are stored locally on your device in private app storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings removes all local data.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app uses third-party SDKs that may collect and process technical and usage information as described in their own privacy policies. All network communication is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.

Advertising — Google AdMob and mediation partners

In the free version, the app displays ads through Google AdMob and AdMob mediation partners. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and mediation partners may automatically collect and share:

  • your device's IP address (used to estimate approximate location),
  • device or other IDs (Android advertising ID AD_ID, App Set ID),
  • app interaction data (such as app launches, feature taps, and ad views),
  • diagnostic information (such as SDK and app performance metrics).

Partners that may serve ads via AdMob mediation:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • AppLovin — applovin.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network — facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Unity Ads — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Liftoff (Vungle) — liftoff.io/privacy-policy
  • Mintegral — mintegral.com/en/privacy
  • Pangle — pangleglobal.com/privacy

Analytics, crash reporting and configuration — Google Firebase

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: anonymized app usage events (e.g., protection toggled, wheel spun) and ad revenue events linked to an app-instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports including stack traces, device model, OS version, and an installation identifier to fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Firebase Remote Config: technical parameters (country, language code, time zone, OS and app version) to deliver app configuration updates.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent & Your Choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states): Before ads are requested in applicable regions, the app displays a consent form via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can accept, decline, or manage your preferences at any time in Settings → Privacy settings for ads (shown where required).
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in Android Settings (Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads).
  • Remove ads: Upgrading to Premium removes all advertisements in the app.

In-App Purchases

Optional Premium subscriptions and lifetime access are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details; the app only verifies your purchase status with Google Play.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention & Security

Third-party SDK data is encrypted in transit and retained according to Google and each partner's respective privacy policies. To delete local app data, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
