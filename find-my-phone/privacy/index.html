Privacy Policy

Find My Phone Anti Theft Alarm (Android app, package com.bebrasoftai.findmyphone). Effective date: October 25, 2026. Русская версия

This Privacy Policy explains how Find My Phone Anti Theft Alarm ("the app", "we", "our") handles your information. Developed by BebraSoftAI. Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com.

Overview

We believe in absolute privacy. The app does not require registration or personal accounts. We do not sell your personal data.

Audio and Microphone Processing

To detect claps and whistles when searching for your phone, the app requests Microphone permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) and runs a foreground service with microphone type (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE).

  • On-Device Ephemeral Processing: Audio captured by the microphone is processed strictly in real-time in the device's volatile memory (RAM) via an on-device fast Fourier transform (FFT) algorithm.
  • Zero Recording or Transmission: The app does not record, save, store, or transmit any audio, voice, or sound recordings. Raw audio data is discarded immediately after spectral analysis.

Device Sensors & Anti-Theft Guard

The Anti-Theft motion detection feature uses the device accelerometer to detect unexpected movement or unplugging from the charger. Sensor readings are evaluated locally and never leave your device.

Data Kept on Your Device

Your sensitivity preferences, chosen alert sounds, quiet hours, and daily lucky wheel progress are stored locally on your device in private app storage. Deleting the app or clearing app storage removes all local data.

Analytics & Diagnostics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to monitor app stability and basic feature interactions (e.g., whether detection is started or stopped). Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymous crash logs to help us fix errors. All network traffic is encrypted via HTTPS.

In-App Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade is processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment details or credit card information.

No Ads

The app contains no third-party advertising networks or ad trackers.
