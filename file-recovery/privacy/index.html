BebraSoftAI / File Recovery Privacy

Privacy Policy — File Recovery

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

1. On-Device File Scanning & Recovery

File Recovery scans storage to locate deleted photos, videos, audio, and documents. Its core function is listing items in the Android system trash (MediaStore rows with IS_TRASHED) and comparing selected folders with the media index.

  • Local Processing Only: All file detection, previewing, and recovery operations occur strictly on your device. Your photos, videos, and files are never uploaded, collected, or shared with our servers or any third parties.
  • Safe File Handling: Recovered files are copied into designated FileRecovery directories and never overwrite existing files.
  • No Root / No Manage All Files: The app operates without root privileges and without requesting dangerous MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions.

2. Media Permissions

The app requests media access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO) solely to scan for recoverable files across system trash locations. Without these permissions, only partial scanning or manually picked folders can be accessed.

3. Third-Party SDKs (Ads & Diagnostics)

The app incorporates standard mobile SDKs:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Displays ads in the free version. AdMob may process device identifiers (Advertising ID) and approximate location to serve and measure non-intrusive ads.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): Collects anonymous operational metrics, device models, and crash reports to ensure app stability.
  • Google Play Billing: Purchases (such as Remove Ads) are processed exclusively by Google Play. We never handle financial card data.

4. Smart Sort (On-Device ML)

Photo sorting uses on-device Google ML Kit Image Labeling. Thumbnail categorization runs completely locally; no images or tags are ever transmitted over the network.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We store no personal data on external servers. All temporary scan caches are deleted when you clear app data or uninstall the app.

6. Contact Us

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, email us at bebrasoftai@gmail.com.
