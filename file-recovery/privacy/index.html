BebraSoftAI / AI File Recovery Privacy

Privacy Policy — AI File Recovery

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

1. AI & Local Processing Architecture

AI File Recovery leverages assistive artificial intelligence algorithms and deep file signature analysis to detect, reconstruct, and restore deleted photos, videos, audio recordings, and documents from Android storage caches and system trash directories.

  • 100% On-Device AI: All artificial intelligence models, heuristic scans, and image thumbnail reconstruction algorithms run strictly on your local device hardware.
  • Zero Cloud Upload of User Files: Your personal photos, videos, audios, and documents are never uploaded to the cloud, never transmitted over the internet, and never shared with third parties or AI service providers.
  • No AI Model Training: Your files and personal data are never used to train, fine-tune, or improve any machine learning models.
  • No Root / No Invasive Permissions: The app operates without root privileges and strictly without dangerous MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions.

2. Media Permissions

The app requests media access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO) solely to scan for recoverable files across system trash locations and user-selected storage directories. Without these permissions, only partial scanning or manually picked folders can be analyzed.

3. Third-Party SDKs (Ads & Diagnostics)

The app incorporates standard, trusted mobile SDKs:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Displays ads in the free version. AdMob may process device identifiers (Advertising ID) and approximate location (derived from IP address) to serve non-intrusive ads in accordance with Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): Collects anonymous operational metrics, device hardware models, and crash reports to maintain app stability.
  • Google Play Billing: Purchases (such as Remove Ads) are processed exclusively by Google Play. We never handle credit card or financial billing information.

4. On-Device Smart Sort (Assisted Machine Learning)

Media categorization uses Google ML Kit on-device Image Labeling. Thumbnail classification and duplicate detection run entirely offline on your phone; no images or tags are ever transmitted to any external server.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not store any personal user files or scanning records on external servers. All temporary scan caches and file indices are completely deleted when you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

6. Contact Us

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at bebrasoftai@gmail.com.
