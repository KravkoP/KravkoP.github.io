Privacy Policy — ThemeMix

Effective date: November 6, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

1. About the app

ThemeMix ("the app") is a home-screen personalization app providing curated wallpapers, aesthetic app icon shortcuts, and interactive Glance widgets. The app operates locally on your Android device without requiring user accounts or logins.

2. Information the app handles locally

  • Theme settings & preferences: Selected theme packs, custom wallpaper configurations, widget settings, lucky wheel unlocks, and day/night auto-switching schedules are stored strictly on your device using local storage.
  • Wallpaper application (SET_WALLPAPER): The app sets home and lock screen wallpapers locally on your request via Android's WallpaperManager. Your wallpapers or device photos are never uploaded or transmitted to any server.
  • Pinned shortcuts (INSTALL_SHORTCUT): Creating aesthetic icon shortcuts uses standard Android launcher APIs locally. We do not track or monitor which third-party apps you launch.
  • Purchases: Pro subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or billing details.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.

3. Third-party services

The app integrates standard mobile SDKs for monetization, diagnostics, and app performance:

3.1 Advertising — Google AdMob and mediation partners

To display non-intrusive full-screen, native, and rewarded ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and certified mediation partners may collect:

  • device IP address (for general location estimation),
  • advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID, App Set ID),
  • app interaction events and ad performance metrics.

Partners that may serve ads in the app:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • AppLovin — applovin.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network — facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Unity Ads — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Liftoff (Vungle) — liftoff.io/privacy-policy
  • Mintegral — mintegral.com/en/privacy
  • Pangle — pangleglobal.com/privacy

3.2 Analytics, crash reporting and configuration — Google Firebase

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: anonymous aggregate app usage events and engagement metrics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash logs, stack traces, and device hardware models to troubleshoot stability.
  • Firebase Remote Config: technical configuration (country code, app version) to tune app parameters dynamically.

4. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states)

For users in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states, a certified Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form is displayed prior to ad requests. You can update your consent choices at any time via Settings → Privacy Options in the app.

5. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Pro access: Upgrading to Pro disables interstitial, app open, and native ads.
  • Local data: Clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to erase all locally saved themes.

6. Children

The app is intended for general audiences aged 18 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and security

We do not store your personal information on our own servers. SDK data transmission is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and relevant privacy frameworks, you have rights to access, delete, or limit processing of personal information. Inquiries may be sent to bebrasoftai@gmail.com.

9. Changes

Any future revisions to this policy will be posted directly to this URL with a revised effective date.
