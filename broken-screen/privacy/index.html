Privacy Policy — Broken Screen Prank

Effective date: October 14, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

1. About the app

Broken Screen Prank ("the app") is an entertainment app that displays a simulated cracked-screen image as a harmless joke. The app does not damage your device, does not change any system settings and does not access your files beyond what you explicitly choose.

2. Information the app itself handles

  • Settings and preferences (sound, vibration, selected crack style, language, onboarding status, lucky-wheel coins and won items, ad frequency counters) are stored only on your device.
  • Photos: if you choose a background photo, you pick it with Android's system photo picker. The photo is used only on your device as the prank background. It is never uploaded or shared.
  • Purchases: the "Premium" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase.
  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, microphone, camera or messages.

3. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect and process information as described in their own privacy policies.

3.1 Advertising — Google AdMob and mediation partners

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and, through AdMob mediation, partner ad networks may automatically collect and share:

  • your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location),
  • advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID),
  • app interaction data (such as app launches, taps and ad views),
  • diagnostic information (such as app and SDK performance).

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention and is encrypted in transit.

Partners that may serve ads in the app:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • AppLovin — applovin.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network — facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Unity Ads — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Liftoff (Vungle) — liftoff.io/privacy-policy

3.2 Analytics, crash reporting and configuration — Google Firebase

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: app usage events (for example, which features are used) and ad revenue events, linked to an app-instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports, including stack traces, device metadata (model, OS version) and an installation identifier.
  • Firebase Remote Config: technical data such as country and language code, time zone, OS version and app version, used to adjust app settings without an update.

Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy laws, the app shows a consent message, provided through Google's User Messaging Platform, before any ad is requested. You can accept, decline, or choose specific purposes and partners. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the app (shown where required).

5. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Consent: use Settings → Privacy options in the app, where available.
  • Ads: purchase "Premium" to remove banner, interstitial, app open and native ads. Optional rewarded videos stay available if you choose to watch them.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and security

We do not operate our own servers for the app and do not store your personal data. Third-party services retain data according to their own policies. Data sent by the SDKs is encrypted in transit.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, most requests are best directed to the third-party services listed above. You can also contact us at bebrasoftai@gmail.com and we will help.
