Privacy Policy — Document PDF Reader & Viewer

Effective date: November 3, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

This policy explains what the Android app Document PDF Reader & Viewer ("the App") does with information. In short: your documents never leave your device; the free version shows ads, which use the Advertising ID.

1. Your documents stay on your device

  • The App opens only the PDF files you choose yourself: in the Android file picker, or with "Open with" from another app (file manager, mail, messenger). It does not ask for storage permissions and does not search your phone for files.
  • Pages are drawn on your device. The contents of your documents (text, images, metadata) are never uploaded, collected or shared by the App.
  • Search inside a document (Android 15 and newer) runs on your device; the words you search for are not sent anywhere.
  • Password-protected PDFs: the password you type is used only to open the file on your device. It is not stored or sent.

2. What the App stores on your device

  • A list of documents you opened: file name, size, number of pages, the system link that lets the App open the file again, the last page you read, whether you starred it, and when you last opened it.
  • A small preview image of the first page of each opened document, for the grid view.
  • Your settings (theme, night mode, screen orientation, sort order, language) and simple counters (for example, how many documents you have read) used to time the rating request and ads.
  • This information stays in the App's private storage. The list of documents and the previews are excluded from Android backups.

3. Sharing and printing

  • When you tap Share, the file is passed to the app you pick in the Android share menu. When you tap Print, the file is passed to Android's print service and the printer or "Save as PDF" option you choose. This happens only on your action.
  • "Share the app" sends a text with a link to the App's Google Play page through the app you pick.

4. Information collected by third-party services

  • Google AdMob and its mediation partners (AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize, Mintegral, Pangle) show ads in the free version. They may collect your device's IP address (to infer an approximate region), the Advertising ID, the App Set ID, device and app information, and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. They do not receive your documents.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) collects app-instance identifiers, app usage events (for example, that a document was opened, without its name or contents), device information and crash reports, to keep the App stable and improve it.
  • Google Play Billing handles the one-time "Remove ads" purchase. We receive only the purchase status; we never see your payment details.
  • Google Play In-App Review may show Google Play's rating card. Your rating and review go to Google Play; the App does not see them.
  • Each service processes data under its own privacy policy: Google (policies.google.com/privacy) and the policies of the mediation partners listed above.

5. Ads and your consent

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where it is required see Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings inside the App, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • The "Remove ads" purchase turns off all ads in the App.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • We have no user accounts, profiles or servers that store your data. To delete the list of recent documents, use Settings → Clear recent files (favorites stay) or remove an item from the list. To delete everything the App stores, clear the App's storage in Android settings or uninstall the App.
  • Data collected by the third-party services above is kept according to their own policies.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
