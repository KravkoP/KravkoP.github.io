Privacy Policy — AI Private Browser

Effective date: November 6, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

This policy explains what the Android app "AI Private Browser" (package com.bebrasoftai.privatebrowser, "the App") does with data.

1. What stays on your phone

  • The App keeps no browsing or search history. Open tabs and their previews exist in memory only.
  • Stored on the phone: bookmarks and their icons, settings, the list of sites where you turned ad blocking off, one number counting blocked items, downloaded files, and the cookies and site data of normal tabs.
  • Cookies and site data of private tabs are deleted when the last private tab closes, or at the next start if the App was killed.
  • Backups of the App's data are turned off.

2. What websites and others can see

The App is not a VPN and does not make you anonymous. Websites you open receive your IP address and the requests needed to load them. Your internet provider and network owner can see which sites you open. Search engines keep their own records under their own policies. Google is the default search engine; you can change it in Settings.

Bookmark icons are fetched directly from each bookmarked site, which sees a request from your IP address.

Android System WebView may use Google Safe Browsing to check pages against a list of dangerous sites.

The App downloads updated EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists from easylist.to every few days. Only the request itself is sent.

3. AI page assistant

Nothing is sent unless you open the assistant and agree. When you do:

  • The visible text of the page (up to 12,000 characters), its title and your questions are sent to our server (Cloudflare Workers) and to the Google Gemini API;
  • The page address is not sent, and the assistant does not read pages with a password field;
  • Our server does not store this text;
  • We use Gemini's paid tier: Google does not use the text and answers to improve its products, and keeps them for a limited time to detect violations of its policies and as the law requires. See https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms;
  • Avoid using the assistant on pages with personal or confidential information;
  • The assistant is not offered in countries where Google does not provide the Gemini API.

You can report an AI answer in the App. If you choose to report by email, the answer goes to us by email.

4. Ads, purchases and analytics

The App shows ads from Google AdMob in its own screens. AdMob and its partners may collect the advertising ID, device data and approximate location from your IP address; in regions where it is required you are asked for consent, and you can change it in Settings. Ads are not inserted into websites.

Purchases are processed by Google Play.

The App may use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for usage events and crash reports. These never include the pages you open, your searches or the text you send to the assistant.

5. Permissions

The App asks for no storage, location, camera or microphone permission. Requests from websites for location, camera and microphone are refused.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

7. Deleting your data

Settings > Clear browsing data removes cookies, site data and cache. Uninstalling the App removes everything it stored, including downloaded files. We hold no account for you.
