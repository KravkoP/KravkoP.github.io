Privacy Policy — LED Flash Alerts Call Messages

Effective date: November 3, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

LED Flash Alerts Call Messages ("the App") blinks your phone's camera flash when someone calls and when the apps you choose post a notification. This policy explains what the App uses, what stays on your phone and what third-party services receive.

1. What the App uses on your phone

  • Phone state (READ_PHONE_STATE). Used only to learn that the phone is ringing, so the flash blinks until the call is answered, declined or missed. The App does not read phone numbers, contacts or the call log, and does not store or send the call state.
  • Notification access. If you turn it on, Android tells the App when a new notification is posted. To decide whether to blink, the App looks only at which app posted it, its category and system flags (for example, whether it is an ongoing or silent notification). The App does not read, store or send the title or text of notifications.
  • Your installed apps. The "Choose apps" screen shows apps that have a launcher icon so you can pick which ones make the flash blink. The list is read on the phone; only the package names you choose are saved in the App's settings on the phone. The list is never sent to us or to third parties.
  • Camera flash. The App switches the torch on and off through Android's camera service. It does not ask for the camera permission, does not open the camera, and does not take photos or record video.
  • Device conditions. To follow your "When not to flash" settings, the App checks on the phone whether the screen is on, the ringer mode, Do Not Disturb, the battery level and charging state, and the time. These checks are not stored or sent.

2. Data stored on your phone

The App saves your settings (alert switches, pattern, timing, brightness, chosen apps, quiet hours) and a few counters (number of launches, when an ad or a rating request was last shown) in its private storage on the phone. We do not have servers or user accounts and do not receive this data.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the App's settings in your device backup in your Google account (Android Auto Backup). This backup is managed by Google under your account; we cannot access it. Ad frequency counters are excluded from backup.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following services. They may collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and mediation partners — AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, Liftoff (Vungle), Mintegral and Pangle. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, they may collect the IP address (used to estimate approximate location), the Advertising ID and App Set ID, device and app information, and ad interactions. Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903 (partners' policies are linked from there).
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — shows the consent form where the law requires it and stores your choice on the phone.
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config (Google) — app-instance identifiers, usage events (for example, that a test flash was run or a screen was opened), crash reports and device information, used to improve stability and adjust settings such as ad frequency remotely. Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — handles the optional "Remove ads" purchase. We do not receive your card or payment details.
  • Google Play In-App Review — may show Google Play's rating card. Your rating is sent to Google Play; the App is not told whether or how you rated it.

4. Sharing and links

  • Invite a friend opens Android's share menu with a short text and a Google Play link. You choose the app and the person; we do not see who you share with.
  • Rate the app opens the App's page in Google Play.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share the data described in section 1 with anyone.

5. Your choices

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where it is required see a consent form for ads. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices on the App's main screen (shown where it applies).
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • You can turn off notification access or the phone permission at any time in Android Settings. The App then stops blinking for notifications or calls.
  • Buying "Remove ads" turns off all ads in the App.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stored by the App stays on your phone until you clear the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the App. Data collected by the third-party services above is kept according to their policies. To ask about data, contact us at the address below.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Data sent by the services above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
