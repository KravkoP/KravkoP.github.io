Privacy Policy — LED Flash Alerts Call Messages

Effective date: October 28, 2026

1. On-Device Flashlight Control & Call Detection

  • Flashlight & Camera Hardware (CAMERA, FLASHLIGHT): Used solely to control the physical LED torch hardware on the back of your phone to create customizable light strobe blinking patterns. The App does not take photos, record video, or access camera images.
  • Incoming Event Detection: Uses standard Android system broadcast receivers to detect when the phone is ringing or receiving an SMS/app notification to trigger the flash alert. No message text, contact names, phone numbers, or conversation content are ever read, saved, or uploaded.
  • Battery & Do Not Disturb Controls: Battery status checks and quiet hours scheduling operate entirely on-device.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party SDKs

The App integrates standard industry-leading third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and Google Play billing:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & Mediation Partners: May collect your device's IP address (to infer approximate geographic region), Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), App Set ID, device technical specifications, and ad interaction events to serve relevant ads, measure impressions, and protect against fraudulent traffic.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): Collects anonymous app-instance identifiers, crash stack traces, and non-personal operational metrics to ensure app performance and stability.
  • Google Play Billing: All in-app purchases and subscriptions are handled directly and securely through Google Play. We do not store or process your financial card or bank details.

3. Links to Our Other Apps

The App may include an optional link or recommendation for our other apps (such as Danger Radar or Hidden Camera Detector). Tapping the link opens Google Play Store. No personal, usage, or sensor data is transferred.

4. Consent & Privacy Choices (GDPR / UK / US States)

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and relevant US states are presented with a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. You can review or modify your consent choices at any time via Settings → Privacy Options inside the App, or by resetting your Advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts, profiles, or remote databases. You can remove all locally stored app data and preferences at any time by clearing the App's storage via Android Settings or by uninstalling the App.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or local legal minimum age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
