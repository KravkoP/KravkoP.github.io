BebraSoftAI / Emoji Battery

Privacy Policy — Emoji Battery

Effective date: November 8, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

1. About the app

Emoji Battery ("the app") lets you personalize how your battery looks: a custom status bar with a battery emoji, themes, stickers, a charging animation, pets that walk on your screen, home screen widgets and an optional Battery Buddy widget shared with a friend.

2. Accessibility service

The custom status bar, stickers, charging animation and on-screen pets are drawn with Android's Accessibility service. The app asks you to turn it on only after an in-app explanation, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in Settings → Accessibility.

  • We use the service only to display the app's own decorations on top of your screen.
  • The service does not read the content of your screen or other apps, your messages, passwords, notifications or anything you type. It is configured so that it cannot retrieve window content.
  • No data is collected, stored or shared through the Accessibility service.
  • While Emoji Battery shows an ad, its status bar steps aside, and the on-screen pets hide while the app itself is open.

3. Information the app handles locally

  • Battery level & charging status: read from standard Android battery broadcasts on your device to draw the status bar, widgets and charging animation. It leaves your phone only if you use Battery Buddy (section 4).
  • Settings & unlocks: chosen skins, themes, stickers, pets, favorites, sounds, language and prize wheel unlocks are saved on your device.
  • Purchases: subscriptions, one-time purchases and spin packs are handled by Google Play Billing. We never see or store payment details.
  • No account is needed. The app does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

4. Battery Buddy (optional)

If you create or join a pair with a friend's code, the app sends the following to our database on Google Cloud Firestore, so that the person who has your code can see it:

  • the nickname you enter (up to 24 characters),
  • your selected pet, battery level, charging state and pet mood,
  • the time of the last update and of the last "charge your phone" zap.

There is no account and no other identifier: the random pair code is the only key. Nothing is sent unless you pair. When you unpair in the app, your record is deleted from the server. If you uninstall the app without unpairing, the record can stay on the server; email us with your pair code and we will delete it.

5. Third-party services

5.1 Advertising — Google AdMob and mediation partners

The app shows full-screen ads (app open, interstitial and optional rewarded videos); it shows no banners. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and partner ad networks may collect:

  • device IP address (for approximate location),
  • advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID, App Set ID),
  • app interaction events and ad delivery telemetry.

Partners that may serve ads in the app:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • AppLovin — applovin.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network — facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Unity Ads — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Liftoff (Vungle) — liftoff.io/privacy-policy
  • Mintegral — mintegral.com/en/privacy
  • Pangle — pangleglobal.com/privacy

5.2 Analytics, crash reporting, configuration and database — Google Firebase

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: app usage and engagement events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash traces and device metrics to fix errors.
  • Firebase Remote Config: technical settings (for example, ad frequency).
  • Cloud Firestore: Battery Buddy data only, as described in section 4.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states)

Users in applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, US states) are shown a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the app.

7. Your choices

  • Accessibility service: turn it off in the app or in Android Settings → Accessibility; the widgets keep working.
  • Battery Buddy: unpair at any time to delete your data from the server.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Pro access: Pro turns off app open and interstitial ads.
  • Local data: clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to erase everything saved on your device.

8. Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention and security

Battery Buddy data is kept only while the pair exists (see section 4). Data sent by the app and its SDKs is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and other applicable privacy laws, you have the right to access, delete or object to processing of your personal data. Contact us at bebrasoftai@gmail.com.
