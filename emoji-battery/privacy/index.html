BebraSoftAI / Emoji Battery

Privacy Policy — Emoji Battery

Effective date: November 6, 2026
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

1. About the app

Emoji Battery ("the app") is a battery indicator and pet widget application that displays expressive emoji characters reacting to your phone's battery level and charging status. The app uses standard Android battery broadcasts and Glance widgets, with zero usage of invasive Accessibility services.

2. Information the app handles locally

  • Battery level & charging status: The app monitors battery percentage, temperature, and power connection via standard local Android system broadcasts (Intent.ACTION_BATTERY_CHANGED, ACTION_POWER_CONNECTED) strictly in memory on your device to update widgets and the charging show. This data is never sent to remote servers.
  • Settings & unlocks: Selected emoji skins, pet emotions, sound preferences, and lucky wheel unlocks are saved locally on your device.
  • DIY photo battery: If you use custom photo frames for your battery widget, the images are picked via Android's photo picker and stored only in your local app storage.
  • Purchases: Pro subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled directly by Google Play Billing. We never collect or store payment details.
  • We do not require account registration, and we do not collect contacts, microphone, camera, or location.

3. Third-party services

The app uses industry-standard third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, and diagnostics:

3.1 Advertising — Google AdMob and mediation partners

To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and partner ad networks may collect:

  • device IP address (for approximate location),
  • advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID, App Set ID),
  • app interaction events and ad delivery telemetry.

Partners that may serve ads in the app:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • AppLovin — applovin.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network — facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Unity Ads — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Liftoff (Vungle) — liftoff.io/privacy-policy
  • Mintegral — mintegral.com/en/privacy
  • Pangle — pangleglobal.com/privacy

3.2 Analytics, crash reporting and configuration — Google Firebase

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: anonymous aggregate app usage and engagement telemetry.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash traces and device metrics to resolve technical errors.
  • Firebase Remote Config: technical configuration parameters (e.g. ad frequency intervals).

4. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states)

Users in applicable jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, US states) are presented with a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog on app launch. Preferences can be updated at any time in Settings → Privacy Options.

5. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Pro access: Upgrading to Pro disables banner, interstitial, and app open ads.
  • Local data: Clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to erase all saved preferences.

6. Children

The app is intended for general audiences aged 18 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and security

We do not operate servers storing user data. Data sent by SDKs is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR and applicable privacy legislation, you have the right to access, delete, or object to processing of personal data. Contact us at bebrasoftai@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
