Privacy Policy — Measure App: Camera & Ruler

Effective date: November 26, 2026
Package: com.bebrasoftai.ruler
Developer: BebraSoftAI
Contact: bebrasoftai@gmail.com

1. About the app

Measure App: Camera & Ruler ("the app", "we", "our") provides augmented reality (AR) camera measurements (line, polyline, height, area, angle), photo measurement with a reference object, an on-screen 1D/2D ruler, a protractor, and a bubble level. No account creation, registration, or login is required.

2. Camera, AR & local processing

  • Camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely to display the live camera preview and compute AR measurements on your device. Camera frames are processed locally in real time and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Google Play Services for AR (ARCore): Live AR measurement uses Google ARCore, provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Photo Measure: When you import an image for Photo Measure, the app uses Android's system Photo Picker so it receives access only to the single image you choose. Imported images are processed locally on your device and never uploaded.
  • Device motion sensors: The protractor, bubble level, and AR tracking read built-in accelerometer and gyroscope sensor data locally in real time. Sensor data never leaves your device.
  • Exported images: When you tap Save Photo, annotated measurement images are saved locally to your device gallery (Pictures/Ruler) or shared via the Android system share sheet only when you choose to share.

3. Information saved on your device

  • Measurement history & folders: Saved measurements, labels, thumbnails, and custom folders are stored locally in private app storage on your device.
  • Preferences & calibration: Default measurement units, screen ruler calibration, daily free AR counter, and UI preferences are stored locally via Android DataStore.
  • In-app purchases: Optional Pro subscriptions and lifetime upgrades are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details.
  • No personal data servers: We do not operate our own server storing your photos, measurements, or personal profiles, and the app does not request device GPS location permissions, contacts, or microphone access.

4. Third-party services

The app integrates trusted Google SDKs:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): The free version displays full-screen ads (app open, interstitial, and opt-in rewarded) and optional native ads; the app contains no banner ads. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device Advertising ID, App Set ID, IP address (for approximate geographic location), app interaction events, and diagnostics.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): Collects aggregate feature usage events, crash stack traces, device model/OS diagnostics, and fetches remote configuration parameters (such as ad pacing and free daily limit defaults).
  • Google Play Services (Billing & In-App Review): Handles purchases, subscription status restoration, and optional Google Play review prompts.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states)

Where required by law (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states), the app presents a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. You can review or change your privacy and ad consent choices at any time in Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent Options.

6. Your choices

  • Camera permission: You can revoke camera access at any time in Android Settings; Screen Ruler, Protractor, Bubble Level, and Photo Measure continue to work without camera permission.
  • Advertising ID: Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Pro access: Upgrading to Pro removes all ads in the app.
  • Local data: Delete individual measurements or folders inside History, clear app storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app to erase all locally stored data.

7. Children

Measure App: Camera & Ruler is intended for general adult audiences aged 18 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and security

Locally saved measurements and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Data transmitted by Google AdMob, Firebase, and Google Play SDKs is encrypted in transit via TLS/HTTPS and retained according to Google's policies.

9. Your rights

Under GDPR and applicable privacy laws, you may request access, correction, deletion, or object to processing of personal data. Contact us at bebrasoftai@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
